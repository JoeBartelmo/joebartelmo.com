My total kitchen gut and remodel. The design phase was difficult becuase i had to clear away the old wall, to add insulation into the wall cavity, there originally was none in the whole house so I took the opportunity to add some while I had no cabinets. I had to make a lot of measurements to make sure everythign would fit perfectly. I only had to take off one day of work, and was ablt to knock this out over a total of 4 days (demolition, removal of the wall, putting up the new wall and subfloor, then installation of the insulation, cabinets and electrical). 

I also took the opportunity to repaint the whole main floor for a refresh. On day 5 the countertop installers came to measure, and i had to wait 2 weeks for it to be cut and installed. Then i finikitchen it off in little pieces (thigns like backsplash i had to order, and that took a while). To be honest, the most annoying part of this whole build was reusing the original hole i made for the microwave 5 years ago. I got lucky and it was almost totally lined up with where it was supposed to be... almost. I had to build a custom vent piece to connect the microwave through the cabinet.

## The List

| Status | Category | Task | Time | Difficulty | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ✅ | 🧱 Demolition | Remove old cabinets and prep the room | 1-2 days | Medium | This was the first big step and made the space feel like a blank slate. |
| ✅ | 🏗️ Wall Work | Remove the old wall and add insulation | 1 day | Medium | The wall had to come down so the space could be insulated and the layout could be cleaned up. |
| ✅ | 🔨 Framing | Rebuild the wall and subfloor area | 1 day | Medium | This was a cleanup step that made the new layout consistent and solid. |
| ✅ | ⚡ Electrical | Rework wiring and fit the cabinet layout | 1 day | Medium | I had to make sure the microwave vent and cabinet spacing would still line up properly. |
| ✅ | 🏗️ Cabinets | Install the new cabinetry and finish details | 2 days | Medium | The main visual change came from the new white finish and tighter fit of the layout. |
| ✅ | 🔧 Countertops | Measure and install the countertop | 2 weeks total | Medium | There was a waiting period for fabrication, and it was one of the final big parts of the project. |
| ✅ | 🎨 Finish | Paint, trim, and final cleanup | 2-3 days | Easy | The room felt dramatically lighter and brighter once the new white finish went in. |
